# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
summarized and reworded for this project. Where this text is unclear, the intent of
the Contributor Covenant 2.1 applies.

## Our commitment

Everyone who participates in ProudAuth — maintainers, contributors, reviewers,
reporters and users — should be able to do so without harassment, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation. We aim for a community that is open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in disagreement; critique ideas and code, not
  people.
- Accept constructive feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and learn from them.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users, including the people whose
  identities and data this software protects.
- Report security issues privately ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) instead of
  disclosing them in public.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Insults, derogatory comments, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, including sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information (such as a physical or email address)
  without their explicit permission.
- Testing, probing or attacking deployments, accounts or data you are not
  authorized to access, including other participants' systems.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, chat and events — and when someone officially represents the project
elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainers at **`conduct@proudauth.example`** —
**placeholder, to be configured by the maintainers before the project accepts
outside contributions.** Until a monitored address exists, contact a member of
the `@proudauth/maintainers` team privately. Reports are handled
confidentially; maintainers who are involved in a report recuse themselves.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may
remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that
do not align with this code. Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant 2.1
enforcement ladder:

1. **Correction** — a private, written explanation of the problem; a public
   apology may be requested.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behavior, including no
   interaction with the people involved for a period of time.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction or public communication with the community
   for a specified period.
4. **Permanent ban** — permanent removal from the community's spaces.
